Where to Stay in Kenya

Here are some great accommodation options in Kenya:

1. Nairobi

For travelers seeking a diverse selection of places to stay, Nairobi offers an extensive range of accommodations, from five-star luxury hotels and boutique properties in vibrant neighborhoods like Westlands and Kilimani to comfortable, budget-friendly options close to the city center..

2. Mombasa

Mombasa is a coastal city with many beachfront hotels and resorts.For an unforgettable stay along the shoreline, consider booking a room at upscale properties like the PrideInn Paradise Beach Resort & Spa Mombasa or the Serena Beach Resort & Spa, both of which combine luxurious amenities, outdoor pools, and direct access to pristine white sands.

3. Kisumu

Nestled along the serene shores of Lake Victoria, Kisumu is Kenya's third-largest city and a vibrant cultural hub in the western region. Known for its relaxed lakeside atmosphere, bustling fish markets specializing in fresh tilapia and Nile perch, and scenic sunsets over the water, it serves as a peaceful gateway to Western Kenya's rich heritage and natural beauty. It offers beautiful scenery and a relaxed atmosphere.
