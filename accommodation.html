Where to Stay in Kenya

Here are some great accommodation options in Kenya:

1. Nairobi

Nairobi offers a wide range of hotels and lodges, from luxury resorts to budget-friendly options.

2. Mombasa

Mombasa is a coastal city with many beachfront hotels and resorts.

3. Kisumu

Kisumu is a lakeside city with several hotels and guesthouses.
